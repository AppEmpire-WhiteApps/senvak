## Description

Senvak turns a walk through your office into a documented, evidence-based Wi-Fi audit.

Stop arguing about "bad Wi-Fi" based on feelings. Walk the floor with Senvak and capture what the network actually does in every room — latency, connection details, speed, photos and notes — point by point.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Create a site — name the office, floor or campus you are auditing.
2. Connect to its Wi-Fi and start a live audit.
3. At each checkpoint, record a measurement: latency to the gateway, current network details (SSID, BSSID, IP, gateway) and an optional internet speed test.
4. Label the room, attach a photo and save.
5. Share the finished audit as a CSV report with your IT team.

KEY FEATURES

• SITES — organize audits by location; keep every office or floor separate.
• LIVE NETWORK SNAPSHOT — see connection state, SSID, BSSID, IP address and gateway in real time.
• LATENCY CHECK — on-device ping to the Wi-Fi gateway for each measurement point.
• INTERNET SPEED TEST — optional download & upload measurement (uses about 7 MB of data).
• AUDIT POINTS — label every room or checkpoint, attach a photo and add notes.
• HISTORY — all captured points stay on your device with their evidence.
• CSV REPORT — export the entire audit and share it with your IT team in one tap.
• PRIVATE BY DESIGN — audit data is stored on-device and leaves it only when you share a report.

WHO IT'S FOR

IT admins and support teams, network engineers, office managers, and anyone responsible for Wi-Fi quality in offices, hotels, schools, clinics, warehouses or coworking spaces.

Senvak keeps the evidence of every audit — so Wi-Fi decisions are made on measurements, not opinions.
